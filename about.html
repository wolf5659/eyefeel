ABOUT US

Feeling Something? Good. That's the Point.

Welcome to EYEFEEL, where feelings run wild and ordinary takes the day off. We create sauces, drinks, apparel, and experiences that spark joy, stir nostalgia, and bring a little madness to your day. We believe brands should be felt.

Why the elephant? In Arabic, "Feel" (فيل) means elephant. In English, it's what you do when something moves you. One word, two languages, one lovable mascot with two perfectly normal eyes. No secret societies or divine powers. We promise it's not that deep.

Bold flavors. Unexpected ideas. Great-quality products made for you. That's EYEFEEL. Sometimes sweet, sometimes strange, always full of imagination. Come curious, question the ordinary, and find something worth feeling.
