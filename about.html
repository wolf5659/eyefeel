ABOUT US

Feeling Something? Good. That's the Point.

Welcome to EYEFEEL, where feelings run wild, creativity knows no limits, and apparently, an elephant needs an explanation.

Let's address the elephant in the room. Literally.

No, our elephant doesn't have one eye.
It has two. Like most elephants. And no, it has absolutely nothing to do with secret societies, mysterious organizations, or whatever conspiracy theory is trending this week.

No, our elephant isn't a deity.
We don't worship elephants. We just happen to like ours. It's a mascot, not a divine being. We promise it's not that deep.

So, why an elephant?

Glad you asked.

In Arabic, "Feel" (فيل) means elephant. In English, "Feel" is what you do when you experience an emotion.

See what we did there?

One word. Two languages. An elephant. A whole lot of feelings.

That's it. That's the big secret.

And what about the name EYEFEEL?

It's about feelings. Emotions. Experiences. The things that make us human.

The joy, the madness, the excitement, the nostalgia, the unexpected, and everything wonderfully strange in between.
